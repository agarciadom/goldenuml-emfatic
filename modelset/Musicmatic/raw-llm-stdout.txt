### Key design decisions:
1. **`User` is abstract** because `BusinessUser` has a `{total}` constraint, meaning every `User` must be either a `RegularUser` or `BusinessUser`.
2. **`{overlap}` on BusinessUser** means a `BusinessUser` can also be a `RegularUser` — this cannot be directly enforced in Ecore but is noted via the inheritance structure.
3. **`{disjoint}` between `Hit` and `Single`** cannot be enforced structurally in Ecore but is respected by keeping them as separate subclasses.
4. **`Track` as association class** between `Hit` and `Album` is modeled as a regular class with references to both.
5. **`compose` relationship** is modeled with `containment="true"` on `RegularUser.composedAlbums`.
