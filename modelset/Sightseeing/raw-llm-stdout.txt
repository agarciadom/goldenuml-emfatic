### Key design decisions:

1. **Root container** (`CityTourismModel`): Created to act as the root, holding `City`, `Person`, `Visitor`, `GuidedTour`, and `Discount` instances that don't have another container.
2. **Inheritance**: `Guide` and `Visitor` both extend `Person` via `eSuperTypes`.
3. **Association class** (`Discount`): Since `Discount` is an association class between `Visitor` and `GuidedTour`, each of those classes references `Discount` rather than directly referencing each other.
4. **Containment**: `City` contains `Attraction` instances (1 city to many attractions). Top-level elements are contained in the root.
5. **Multiplicities**: Upper bounds of `-1` represent `*` (many), and default lower bound of `0` is used unless explicitly required.
